cyberwolfon-device ai

CyberWolf AI Assistant

A voice assistant that knows who is talking, and never touches the cloud.

## What it does

  • Wakes only for its owner: a custom wake-word and voice-signature model, trained from scratch in PyTorch, with over 95% wake-word accuracy in quiet rooms.
  • Checks identity twice. OpenCV face recognition adds a second factor on top of the voice match.

## How it's built

  • faster-whisper transcribes speech locally; Llama 3.2 through LM Studio does the language work.
  • The whole pipeline runs on edge hardware with sub-second end-to-end response.

lumisenseiot

LumiSense

Room lighting that changes colour with the air you're breathing.

## What it does

  • Fuses gas, CO and particulate readings and shifts light colour and intensity in real time.
  • Raises an instant visual and audible alert when gas or CO crosses a hazard threshold.

## How it's built

  • Custom C++ firmware on Arduino. It runs on its own, with no host computer.
  • Publishes readings over MQTT for remote monitoring, while the alert logic stays local.

"stack": ["Arduino", "C++", "MQTT", "sensor fusion"]

Network Security Auditor

Finds who is on a network, and where its access control is weak.
